02 / What a chapter is

A Gateway, Not A Branch.

We don’t open a chapter to chase reach. We open one where young people want to do policy work together, and someone local is willing to lead it.

i. Local leadership

Led by people who live there.

Every chapter is built and run by young people in that place, so its questions, partners and events come from the community it serves.

ii. Real publications

Work published under your name.

Chapters publish policy briefs and explainers through YSP, edited to the same standard across the network and credited to the people who wrote them.

iii. University links

Built around local campuses.

Chapters work with nearby universities and student societies, so research, events and recruitment grow from where young people already are.

iv. A global network

One chapter, the whole network.

Every chapter connects to YSP Global’s mentors, programmes and other chapters, so local work feeds into a wider international conversation.

03 / The founding team

Six Roles In Every Chapter.

Every chapter recruits the same founding roles. All roles are volunteer, fully remote and run on a 16-week founding term that leads into the chapter’s first full cohort.

Honest about the trade. We are not yet funded. What we offer is real work, a real mentor network and a real CV line, not a salary. Apply for the role closest to your skills, and note a second if you’d consider it.

Sixteen Weeks, Then A Real Cohort.

A small, honest schedule that every new chapter follows. The founding term is sixteen weeks; the first full member cohort begins at the end of it.
