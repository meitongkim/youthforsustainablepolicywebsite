Foundational

Programme Charter & Constitution

The structure, principles, and governance framework that guide every YSP programme, decision, and member relationship.

Youth for Sustainable Policy (YSP) is established as a youth‑led, impact‑driven initiative dedicated to advancing sustainable, equitable, and evidence‑based public policy. This Charter defines the structure, principles, and governance framework guiding YSP's programmes, operations, and community.
01

Mission & Vision

Mission. To empower youth to engage with global challenges through research, storytelling, and policy dialogue, while making public policy accessible.

Vision. A future where young people are active, informed participants shaping policy at local, national, and global levels.

02

Core Values

Accessibility

Making policy understandable and inclusive.

Curiosity

Encouraging interdisciplinary exploration.

Integrity

Upholding truth, accuracy, and accountability.

Collaboration

Building a global network of contributors.

Creativity

Using media and storytelling to communicate ideas.

03

Organisational Structure

YSP operates under a structured leadership system. Roles and responsibilities are defined through the Team Directory, weekly check‑ins, and departmental coordination led by the COO.

  • C‑Suite — strategic leadership and oversight.
  • Directors — departmental leadership and execution.
  • Managers — operational delivery and coordination.
  • Members — programme participants and contributors.
04

Programme Framework

YSP runs a 16‑week structured programme across six routes — Research, Policy, Business, Diplomacy, Environment, and Community. Outputs are published or presented under the member's name.

  • Foundations — Weeks 1–4.
  • Deep Dive — Weeks 5–12.
  • Capstone Output — Weeks 13–16.
05

Membership

Eligibility is open to youth aged 16–28. Participation is remote and asynchronous, structured around 5–10 hours per week.

  • Engage actively and responsibly.
  • Contribute meaningful work.
  • Uphold all YSP policies.
06

Governance & Decision‑Making

  • Strategic decisions are led by the C‑Suite.
  • Operational decisions are managed by Directors and Managers.
  • Transparency is maintained through the Announcements channel and documented decisions in Notion.
  • Major decisions are clearly communicated and supported by rationale and evidence.
07

Code Of Conduct & Ethics

All members must act with respect, professionalism, and integrity; engage in evidence‑based discussion; and maintain academic honesty. Violations may result in disciplinary action in accordance with the Code of Conduct & Ethics Policy.

08

Intellectual Property

See the full Intellectual Property Policy.

  • Members retain authorship of their work.
  • YSP retains rights to publish and showcase outputs.
  • All work must be original, properly credited, and ethically produced.
09

Data Privacy & Confidentiality

YSP commits to responsible handling of data in line with PDPA compliance. Members must protect confidential information and use data responsibly and ethically. See the Data Privacy & PDPA Compliance Policy.

10

Operations & Systems

These systems ensure accountability, coordination, and transparency across teams.

  • Weekly Check‑In System.
  • Meetings & Scheduling Framework.
  • Timezone & Availability Tracker.
  • Announcements & Decision Logs.
11

Amendments

  • Amended by C‑Suite approval, with consultation where necessary.
  • Updates are communicated to all members and reflected in official documentation.
By participating in YSP, members agree to uphold this Charter and all associated policies, contribute to a professional, impact‑driven environment, and support YSP's mission and values.
