The Challenge

Hard To Explain, Important To Understand.

Some of the world’s most important policy issues are also among the hardest to communicate.

The YSP Visual Policy Challenge invites young designers, researchers, writers and communicators to transform complex policy ideas into accessible, research-backed visual stories.

Through infographics and educational carousels, participants will make evidence understandable without sacrificing accuracy or nuance.

Our Approach

Design For Understanding.

Effective policy communication does more than capture attention.

It helps audiences understand a problem, recognise relevant evidence, consider different perspectives and identify why an issue matters.

This challenge encourages participants to combine strong research with thoughtful visual design.

Beautiful design matters. Accurate information matters more.

What Can You Create?

Two Formats, One Story.

Format 01

Policy Infographic

A standalone visual explaining a policy issue, research finding or public data pattern.

Format 02

Educational Carousel

A sequence of up to eight slides guiding audiences through a policy topic using text, illustrations, graphics and data.

Submit one of these. Submissions must be accessible, clearly sourced and suitable for an educational audience.

Suggested Themes

Pick An Issue That Matters.

  • Climate change and environmental policy.
  • Education and youth opportunity.
  • Sustainable cities and communities.
  • Healthcare access and public wellbeing.
  • Technology and digital governance.
  • Youth participation in policymaking.

Final theme requirements will be included in the official competition brief.

Proposed Competition Details

The Essentials.

FormatOnline creative challenge
EligibilityAges 16–28
TeamsIndividual or up to three
Window21 days
ExperienceNo professional design experience needed
Submission Requirements

Each Entry Includes.

  • One original infographic, or a carousel of up to eight slides.
  • A 150–250-word design rationale.
  • A list of research sources.
  • Captions or appropriate alternative text for accessibility.
  • Attribution and licensing information for external assets.
  • A declaration of relevant AI assistance, under our draft Research, Authorship & AI policy.
Proposed Judging Criteria

Accuracy First.

Research accuracy35%
Clarity and accessibility30%
Visual communication and design25%
Originality and attribution10%

Entries should balance clarity, accuracy, accessibility and creativity.

What Makes A Strong Submission?

Coherent, Responsible, Clear.

A compelling entry tells a coherent story, uses information responsibly and makes a complicated issue easier for a non-specialist to understand.

It should avoid misleading statistics, oversimplified conclusions and unattributed imagery.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions.

Do I need to be a professional designer?+

No. Students, researchers, illustrators and emerging creatives are welcome under the proposed eligibility rules.

Can I use Canva or other design software?+

Yes. The choice of software will not determine scoring.

Will selected designs be published by YSP?+

Publication may be offered following factual and editorial review, with separate permission from the creators. Submission does not guarantee publication.
