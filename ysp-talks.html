YSP Talks.
Youth, Policy, and the World We're Inheriting.

The YSP Podcast is a youth-led conversation about the policy decisions that define our world. Each episode, we sit down with professionals to break down the ideas behind the policies that matter most to young people.

01 / The show

New Episodes Every Two Weeks.

Each episode, we sit down with researchers, policymakers, and practitioners to break down one policy issue in depth: climate, AI governance, migration, education, and more.

The YSP Podcast is produced by Youth for Sustainable Policy, a global, youth-led non-profit empowering young people aged 16–28 to research and contribute to policy — for free.

02 / The season at a glance

10 Episodes, January Through May.

Conversation or interview

No crew caps, no fixed seats

04 / The crew — open call, no limits

Every Role Is Open To As Many People As Want To Do It.

No "1 scriptwriter per episode" nonsense — if six people want to write questions for Episode 4, six people write questions for Episode 4. Pick your episode, pick your role, show up for that one. That's the whole commitment.

Hosts / Co-Hosts

Lead the actual conversation. One host or two, your call.

Guest Researcher / Episode Lead

Owns the episode: guest, research brief, host prep.

Scriptwriter / Question Writer

Drafts the interview questions or conversation arc.

Audio Editor / Sound Producer

Cleans up the recording, adds intro/outro.

Show Notes & Transcript Writer

Description, key takeaways, timestamps, full transcript.

Social Clips & Promo Team

Short clips for Instagram/TikTok, captions, promo push.

Translation & Subtitling Team

Brand new this season — see below.

Booking & Outreach

Reaches out to and coordinates with guests, scheduling.

Episode Cover Art

Designs the episode-specific visual, in the YSP Talks look.

Listener Engagement

Runs the Discord/Telegram thread, collects listener questions.

05 / Propose a guest

Anyone Can Pitch Who We Should Talk To.

This is genuinely open — you don't have to be signed on as an episode's Guest Researcher to pitch a guest. Anyone can propose one for any open episode. If your pitch lands, you get first right of refusal on running that episode as its Guest Researcher.

We're looking for professionals across the full range of policy work — researchers, policymakers, NGO and IGO staff, journalists covering policy, academics. Doesn't have to be someone famous. Someone doing real, specific work in the area is usually a better episode than someone with a big title and nothing new to say.

Guest pitch template
Who
Name and title/role
Why them
What they actually do, and why it connects to a policy issue young people should understand

The thing you'd actually want to ask them
06 / Translation & subtitling — new this season

Every Episode, In Your Language.

YSP's ambassador base speaks dozens of languages between them, and right now every episode only reaches the people who understand English. That changes this season.

Show notes translation

Translate the episode description and key takeaways into your language

Transcript translation

Full episode transcripts, translated

Subtitles

Timed captions for the video/clip versions

Tell us which language(s) you can translate into when you sign up. You pick which episodes you want to translate — same shift-based system as every other role. No fixed workload, no obligation to do every episode.

If enough people sign up for a language, that language gets a standing "translation lead" who coordinates the others — but that's a "if it makes sense" structure, not a requirement to start.
